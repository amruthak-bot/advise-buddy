# Advise Buddy demo video script — 2 to 3 minutes

Read this over the screen recording. Keep it conversational; do not
read the bracketed stage directions aloud.

## 0:00 — The problem (20 seconds)

[Show the home screen.]
"Every search result tells you something. None of them tells you
what is missing. Advise Buddy starts from the other end: first you
define what a complete answer looks like, then it checks the
evidence against that list."

## 0:20 — The blueprint (30 seconds)

[Load the passport template, then Load demo session.]
"This is the blueprint: nine questions a complete passport answer
must cover — eligibility, documents, fee, portal, appointment,
processing, police verification, renewal, contact. I can edit it,
and the app only ever suggests additions. It never changes the
blueprint on its own."

## 0:50 — The heatmap (40 seconds)

[Run analysis. Show the heatmap.]
"Each row is a question, each column a source. Green is stated,
red is a possible conflict, grey is silent. Click any cell and you
see the exact quote it came from — never a rewritten quote.
Here, the fee row is red: older guides say Rs 1,500, newer guides
say Rs 2,500. That is real — the fee changed on 1 July 2026 under
Gazette notification G.S.R. 516(E), and the older guides were never
updated."

## 1:30 — Silence, freshness, checklist (40 seconds)

[Point to the silence flags, the outdated column, the checklist.]
"Some claims appear only in third-party guides and nowhere in the
official source reviewed. That is flagged as unconfirmed by
official source — silence is not proof that a claim is false.
The 2024 guide is flagged possibly outdated, and undated sources
are labelled undated, never given an invented date. The checklist
then orders what to verify first: conflicts, then official
silence, then gaps. Right now, renewal and contact have no source
at all — that is the evidence debt."

## 2:10 — Closing a gap (30 seconds)

[Add a new source in the verification loop, re-run, show the diff.
Then mark one gap resolved with a note.]
"Add new evidence and the heatmap updates, with changed cells
highlighted and one line saying exactly what changed. A gap closes
automatically only on verified official evidence. Otherwise I close
it myself — but only with a note saying how it was resolved.
Nothing closes silently."

## 2:40 — Close (10 seconds)

"Advise Buddy does not just tell you what you know. It shows you what
you don't know — and what to verify next."
