# GapScope — a 2 to 3 minute walkthrough

Use the deployed app. Keep the source excerpts unchanged and the **illustrative** label visible. Do not claim the example establishes the correct fee or proves a policy change.

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

[Home screen.]

“Before applying for something important, we open several websites. We find information, but still don't know which questions remain unanswered. GapScope turns that uncertainty into a short list of things to verify.”

## 0:20–0:40 — Start simply

[Show the question, briefly open **Customize this check** to reveal the editable questions, then select **See an example**.]

“First, I define what I need to know: documents, fees and timing. That lets GapScope find unanswered questions instead of only summarizing. This passport example is cached so the walkthrough is reproducible. It includes supplied source excerpts and one clearly labelled illustrative older source. For a fresh question, GapScope can search and fetch pages automatically.”

## 0:40–1:10 — Inspect a specific concern

[Open the fee card's **See the source evidence**. Show the exact pair, labels and links.]

“The first check asks me to confirm the fee before paying. These are the exact two quotes behind the signal. The app doesn't choose a fee for me. It tells me why to check official guidance for my application type and date.”

[Open the processing check and its comparison reason.]

“It also avoids an obvious false alarm. Working days and calendar days, or multiple timelines for different services, are not automatically contradictory. Those are labelled as needing context.”

## 1:10–1:35 — Separate information from gaps

[Open **What we found**. Show one quote and source. Then show an **Answer not found** card.]

“The source summary is separate from potential issues. A quote check establishes that these words occur in the text, not that they are true. A missing answer means we didn't find it in the pages we could read—not that the information doesn't exist.”

## 1:35–2:10 — Add information and show the change

[Open **Show more checks** if needed. For eligibility choose **Add information you found**. Title: **Illustrative follow-up — not official guidance**. Keep **Third-party** and paste this workflow example:]

> For this illustrative application, eligible applicants must provide an Example ID.

[Select **Add evidence & update results**. Show the update message. Select **Show more checks** if needed to show eligibility now needing official confirmation.]

“Now there is a source quote for eligibility, so the missing-evidence signal changes. But this is illustrative third-party information. Official confirmation is still needed. Adding text doesn't silently resolve the issue.”

## 2:10–2:30 — Take away a checklist

[Select **Save my checklist**. Show the downloaded report's question, next actions, quotes and source references.]

“I can take this checklist away with the evidence and unresolved questions intact. GapScope helps me see what I still need to verify, why, and where to check.”

## Recording and honesty checks

- Show only the public app and report. Hide keys, account pages, notifications and unrelated tabs.
- Record the actual interaction and narration with readable text; do not present screenshots as a live recording.
- Keep sample labels visible. Never describe illustrative evidence as official.
- A fresh-search segment is optional; keep the whole recording under three minutes and show unread counts honestly.
- Watch the uploaded video and check sound and public access before submitting its link.
